A FREE STARTING POINT

Build a shortlist you can explain.

  1. Start with buyer criteria. Narrow the county, property type, total acquisition budget, and repair tolerance.
  2. Use HCAD bulk data for property context. Mailing differences and ownership duration are research signals. They do not prove motivation, vacancy, or equity.
  3. Review event evidence. Confirm probate authority, tax balances, and whether a trustee sale is still scheduled. Match parcels and documents carefully.
  4. Underwrite a range. Bring supported comparable sales, repair estimates, current payoff information, closing and holding costs, and a real buyer price.
  5. Review the contract and disclosures. An assignment transfers your contractual interest. Record seller and prospective-buyer disclosures and review title before proceeding.
